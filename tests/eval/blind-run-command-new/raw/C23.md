## Application ideas

### Principle #2 Taking Out (Extraction)
- **Move the bulk out of the case.** This applies "pull the disturbing part out of the object". The bulky battery goes into the strap or a clasp module, and the watch head keeps only a thin cell. The band's flat, long shape carries capacity without adding thickness at the wrist.
- **Keep only what's needed on the wrist.** This applies "keep only the part that is truly needed". Power-hungry parts such as GPS or the cellular radio move to a phone or a detachable module, so the main battery only powers the essentials.

### Principle #19 Periodic Action
- **Pulse the sensors.** This applies "replace a continuous action with a periodic pulsed action". Heart-rate and SpO2 sensing sample in short bursts instead of continuously, and the sampling rate adapts to motion.
- **Duty-cycle the display and radio.** This applies "use the pauses between pulses to do something else". The display refreshes at a low rate in always-on mode. During the pauses, Bluetooth syncs in batches and the processor sleeps. This cuts consumption, so a smaller battery can last a week.

### Principle #30 Flexible Shells and Thin Films
- **Use a thin flexible cell.** This applies "use flexible shells or thin films in place of bulky structures". A thin-film or pouch cell follows the curved back of the case, and a laminated pouch replaces the rigid can.

### Principle #10 Preliminary Action
- **Pre-position the energy.** This applies "position objects ahead of time so they act instantly from the most convenient place". A very thin backup or supplementary cell is built into the strap, or a charging cradle keeps the watch topped up. Pre-charging at the desk means less capacity is needed on the wrist.

### Principle #9 Preliminary Anti-Action
- **Counter heat and swelling in advance.** This applies "apply in advance an opposite action that cancels a harmful one to come". Cells swell as they age, so a thin compliance layer or pre-loaded gap absorbs the swelling. The case doesn't need extra headroom, so it can be thinner.
